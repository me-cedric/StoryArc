# UI audit, 2026-10-23

Scope: the 18 catalogue screens in `docs/designs/screen-catalogue.md`, on iOS (`apps/ios`) and
Android (`apps/android`), read from source. Themes: consistency, platform guidelines, reuse,
responsiveness, performance. Method: grep for the patterns the brief names, then read each hit.

## State

- Both shells follow their platform: iOS uses `TabView` with `Tab`, `.sidebarAdaptable`, the glass
  accessory and `GlassEffectContainer`; Android uses `NavigationSuiteScaffold`, `ButtonGroup`,
  flexible top bars and the pane scaffolds. No custom material or shadow on Android.
- Spacing and radius tokens are used almost everywhere. 207 `.dp` literals remain on Android and
  83 size literals on iOS; most are the player, the thumbnail strip and touch targets.
- Colour tokens hold. The reader draws its chrome in literal white on both platforms; Android has a
  design-system answer for that and does not use it.
- Android does not apply the typography tokens. `StoryArcType` has no caller, and the editorial
  face is the system serif, not the bundled Source Serif 4 the contract names.
- Both readers handle a hinge. The iOS device build does not declare multiple scenes.
- Six small components are written twice on each platform: the matte swatch, the hex parser, the
  voice-stopped notice, the reader message, the menu row and the switch row.
- Remote covers are decoded at full size on the main thread on both platforms, in four places each.

## Already recorded

- Cover grid thresholds and the window-class asymmetry (iOS two classes at 600 pt, Android five
  with panes at 840 dp): `docs/design.md` §4, register #4.
- The `coverGap` rename, the detail hero cap, the nav bar colours, the failed-download actions:
  `docs/designs/reviews/2026-09-05-design-review-disposition.md`, rows 1 to 12.
- Open from that review: Android source-detail device frames (row 13), the two missing fixtures
  (row 14), Reduce Transparency and Increase Contrast sweeps (row 15).
- Largest-size faults: entry 08 (the bar cuts the title), 13 (the large title ends in an ellipsis),
  14 (titles under covers end in an ellipsis), 20a and 20b (the banner overlaps the title), and
  Android `01b` ("Next in series" wraps one letter per line): `docs/designs/screen-catalogue.md`.
- `items(matches)` without a key in `PdfTextSheet.kt:196` and `SearchInBook.kt:108`: the comment
  above each gives the reason (duplicate locators).
- The two iOS brightness modifiers are a stated mirror: `ReaderBrightness.swift:26`. This audit
  disputes the reason, not the record.
