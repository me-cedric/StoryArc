# Before the UI revamp — 2026-08-30

Thirteen captures of the app as it stood before
[`ui-revamp-2026-08.md`](../../ui-revamp-2026-08.md) was executed. They are the comparison
baseline for `after-2026-08-30/`, and several of them are the evidence the direction
document itself cites in §7.5:

- **The iPhone had no tab bar at all** — only a floating search pill, with no safe area
  under it, so cover titles at the foot of the grid rendered *behind* it.
- **Six unlabelled icon buttons** in a floating pill at the top of the phone, seven on the
  iPad, with no labels, no grouping and no overflow.
- **`Attic NAS` was a primary navigation destination**, which says *NAS* to a reader.
- **The iPad wasted most of its width**: eight phone-sized cells in a 13-inch window, with
  no section structure.

Nothing here should be edited. These are what the revamp was measured against.
