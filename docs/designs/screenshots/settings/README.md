# The settings screens

Twenty-four captures of Settings and its groups, in both appearances and at the default and
largest text sizes. They predate the dated directories beside them and are kept in one place
because Settings is the one area of the app where the matrix — screen × appearance × text
size — is complete on Android and not on iOS.

`docs/openspec/STATUS.md` names that asymmetry and says explicitly that it is **not** a
deliberate divergence: it is where the tooling happened to get built. Eight Android settings
screens are captured here against three on iOS, and none of the iOS three is at the largest
text size.
