# First run, 2026-08-30

Fifteen captures of what a reader sees before they own anything: the empty states of the
three destinations on both platforms, the add-books path, and the two states a library
falls into when it has sources but nothing to show — `pending`, while a scan is running,
and `away`, when every source is unreachable.

They exist because `sources` states the whole of the first screen in one sentence — "one
sentence in plain language, one primary action that opens a comic from the device with
nothing to configure first, and one plain secondary action that leads to connecting a
library" — and what stood there before was four rows naming four transports, two of them
inert. `before-2026-08-30/` holds that.

`ios-library-empty-dark-largest` and `android-library-empty-dark-largest` are the same
screens at the largest text size, which is where the second action had been unreachable.
