# Linux conformance walk, wave 4

Task 4.22 of `desktop-clients`. This record lists every scenario of the capabilities that wave 4 lane L names: the EPUB and PDF reader, the player and read-aloud. It is the Linux twin of `desktop-conformance-macos-wave4.md` and has the same 92 rows in the same order. It is dated 2026-10-18. It was written from existing evidence before the host pass: the wave 4 headless walk, the accessibility and audio notes, and the code and tests at the head of `main` (`b2d0fc35f`). No test was run and no build was made for this record. Later the same day the 49 failing pieces were built, and the rows were updated from named tests, the container build and two more walks; see *What changed on 2026-10-18*. The host pass fills in the "not proved" rows on a real Linux desktop. The scenarios are read from the specs by name, so a scenario added to a spec later is missing here until the record is updated. For `desktop-experience`, only the requirements the wave 4 tasks name are listed.

| Result | Means |
| --- | --- |
| pass | A named test in `apps/desktop-linux/src` asserts it. |
| seen | A headless walk frame or dump (`docs/designs/screenshots/desktop/linux/wave4/`) shows it. Nothing asserts it. The evidence column says which. |

Totals: pass 48, seen 14, fail 0, not proved 23, n/a 7, of 92 scenarios (updated 2026-10-18; written the same day with 49 failing).

**What the evidence is.**

- The walk (`apps/desktop-linux/scripts/wave4-walk.sh`, run 2026-10-17): the debug build under cage 0.1.5 (headless wlroots, 1280 by 720, pixman) in the Ubuntu 24.04 container (GTK 4.14.5, libadwaita 1.5.0, WebKitGTK 2.52.6, GStreamer 1.24.2). It had a session bus, an accessibility bus and no sound device. `wtype` and `gapplication action` drove it and `grim` captured it.
- What the walk measured: `fixture.epub` reported `ready` and the position *1 of 2*, and its view painted white. That was not the GPU: the host answered the chapters with 404 and its policy blocked the navigator's `blob:` stylesheets. Both were fixed on 2026-10-18 and the page now paints, paginated and themed, in the same container (frames `epub-reader.png` and `epub-paper.png`). `text-pages.pdf` opened with a text pane and an outline, and a search for `chapter two` jumped to page *2 of 3*. `id3-chapters.mp3` played through `playerctl`: status, title, position, a pause and a seek to 2 seconds (`playerctl.txt`).
- Not run: Orca, GNOME, Hyprland, X11, a sound device, the media keys, a voice heard aloud (a null output stood in), the Flatpak runtime and the WebKitGTK 2.44 floor.
- The EPUB surfaces walk (`scripts/wave4-epub-walk.sh`) and the player walk (`scripts/wave4-player-walk.sh`), 2026-10-18: the same container and cage, with speech-dispatcher on libao's null driver for the voice. Their frames and dumps are in the same folder; its README names each one.
- Tests: the crate has 327 tests on 2026-10-18 (291 before). `container-build.sh ubuntu-24.04 --clippy` ran them all and clippy with no warning. The tests that need a display or a speech engine skip there by name, and ran green under cage in the same image: the theme surface, the renderer round trip, the PDF selection, the egress page and the voice. The PDF, display and `playerctl` tests skip by name when PDFium, a display or `playerctl` is missing, and the container build supplies all three.

**What changed on 2026-10-18.** The record was written with 49 rows failing: the EPUB window turned pages and read one chapter aloud, and the player had the compact bar only. The same day the Linux crate gained the theme surface and typography (`epub_look.rs`, `epub_theme_ui.rs`), the contents, marks, search and way back (`epub_marks.rs`, `epub_marks_ui.rs`), a voice that reads the whole book and a full player with chapters, speed and a sleep timer (`player.rs`, `playback.rs`, `audio_chapters.rs`, `player_window.rs`), the audiobook section of the publication page (`detail_view.rs`) and the PDF selection (`pdf_marks.rs`). Building them found three host defects that had kept every EPUB page blank; they are in *Found while reading*. The rows that remain not proved need a real desktop, a real audio device, a larger text size, or a fixture the corpus lacks.

## The failures, in one list

No row fails. The list below says where each group was built.

- **EPUB look and typography:** built 2026-10-18 (`epub_look.rs`, `epub_theme_ui.rs`). Eleven rows pass; three are not proved until a capture of the second depth, a larger text size or a size change mid-chapter exists.
- **EPUB position and navigation:** built 2026-10-18 (`epub_marks.rs`, `epub_marks_ui.rs`). Seven rows pass; two are not proved because the corpus EPUB has no footnote and declares its contents.
- **PDF:** built 2026-10-18 (`pdf_marks.rs`). A selection in the page text offers the five colours, a note, copy and search, kept as the EPUB's record.
- **Read aloud and its transport:** built 2026-10-18 (`player.rs`, `epub_window.rs`). The voice reads the book chapter after chapter from the one in view, its sentence drawn and followed, retaken by a reopened book, stopped with a notice by another. Seven of its nine rows pass and two are seen.
- **Player:** built 2026-10-18 (`playback.rs`, `audio_chapters.rs`, `player_window.rs`, `player_ui.rs`, `detail_view.rs`). The bar leads back, the full player has the chapters, the sleep timer and the speed, and the publication's page lists the chapters. Eleven of its fifteen rows pass, three are seen, and *Headphones removed* needs a real audio device.

**Found while reading the code and the frames.**

- The skip buttons of the bar stay active for a voice and do nothing. Resolved 2026-10-18: for a voice they move by a sentence and say so.
- `EpubReader::received` hands the `href` of a `link` message to the launcher with no scheme check. `epub_view::decide` does check.
- The shelf rows have no name, and the close button of the PDF search bar has no name (dump `a11y-pdf-search.txt` counts 3 controls with no name).
- The frame README says `epub-next.png` shows *2 of 2*. The frame has an empty foot and the same size as `epub-reader.png`. No capture shows the second position. Resolved 2026-10-18: the reader page kept the promise `StoryArcEpub.open` returns rather than the reader, so every host message, `next` included, was dropped. The page now awaits it, and `epub-next.png` shows the second page.
- Found 2026-10-18 and fixed: the renderer asks for a chapter relative to the manifest's folder (`/.storyarc/OEBPS/ch1.xhtml`) and the host answered 404, and the host's policy had no `blob:` for the stylesheets the navigator injects. Together they are why the walk's page painted white.
